01 Specific Client Requirements Matrix

The following technical and functional requirements have been formally commissioned by Talking Tables, located Beside Vignan University Main Gate, Vadlamudi, and architected by Koraxa Technologies:

Req ID Feature Module Client Detailed Specification Classification
FR-01 POS Terminal & Menu Full-featured touch/keyboard billing terminal configured with Talking Tables specific menu (Starters, Biryani, Curries, Rotis, Beverages). Fast search, live cart, quantity toggles, and zero lag. Mandatory
FR-02 KOT Elimination Client explicitly requires NO KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket). Orders must bypass kitchen chit printing and directly finalize into master customer bills with a single click. Excluded by Client
FR-03 Clean Customer Bill (No Payment Options) The printed customer bill MUST NOT show payment mode breakdowns (NO UPI, CASH, or CARD options printed on the bill). The bill must only show Bill No, Date/Time, Items, Quantities, Rates, and Grand Total. Special Constraint
FR-04 Operational Dashboard Real-time managerial overview featuring Today's Gross Sales, Total Bills Issued, Daily Expenses Outflow, and Net Operating Margin (Sales minus Expenses). Mandatory
FR-05 Bill History & Reprint Searchable archive of historical bills by Bill Number or Customer Mobile, with one-click re-print capability for auditing or customer requests. Mandatory
FR-06 Daily Expenses Accounting Integrated expense ledger to record store outflows including Meat (Chicken/Mutton/Fish), Groceries & Vegetables, Staff Salaries & Daily Wages, Commercial LPG Gas, and Maintenance. Mandatory
FR-07 Sales & Expense PDF Report End-of-day audit reports combining food revenue with itemized expenses, producing an executive PDF document with net profit calculation. Mandatory
FR-08 Direct PDF File Download & WhatsApp Send Direct File Attachment (NOT text-only links): When downloading the sales report, the system MUST save the PDF locally to the billing PC AND directly send the actual binary .pdf file with full Talking Tables Branding directly to the owner's WhatsApp self-chat (+91 9346477777). Direct Document
FR-09 Store Settings Manager Self-service configuration for restaurant metadata (Talking Tables, Vadlamudi address, phone), WhatsApp dispatch number, and menu item/price manager. Mandatory

Detailed Analysis of Specialized Exclusions

1. Rationale for KOT Elimination: Fast-casual dining and counter-service models near university campuses (e.g. Vignan University) experience heavy lunch rush surges. Bypassing KOT saves 15-20 seconds per ticket and eliminates kitchen printer maintenance.
2. Rationale for Clean Receipt (No UPI/Cash/Card on Bill): Talking Tables requests a clean, uncluttered invoice for patrons. Omitting payment labels keeps the bill compact, simplifies accounting disputes, and minimizes thermal paper roll consumption.
